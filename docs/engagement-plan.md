# Engagement Planning Template

## Authorization
Document the asset owner, written authorization, approved dates, permitted systems, prohibited systems, and emergency contacts before any testing begins.

## Objectives
Define measurable goals such as validating identity controls, testing a detection hypothesis, or assessing segmentation effectiveness.

## Rules of Engagement
- Use only approved lab targets.
- Do not access real user data.
- Do not persist outside the agreed test window.
- Stop immediately if activity affects non-lab systems.
- Maintain an activity log and evidence chain.

## Success Criteria
A scenario is successful when the team can demonstrate whether preventive and detective controls behaved as expected, document gaps, and produce actionable remediation.

## Evidence
Record timestamps, host roles, ATT&CK technique IDs, telemetry sources, alerts generated, analyst response, and remediation recommendations.
